# KP-400 Horizontal Split-Case Pump — Installation, Operation & Maintenance Manual

Document IOM-KP400, revision B (2025-11).

> The KP-400 is a large double-suction pump, usually mounted on a **flexible (steel-frame) foundation**. Several limits differ from the KP-250 — do not reuse KP-250 values.

## 1. Safety

Follow the lockout/tagout and "never run dry" rules of IOM-KP250 §1; they apply equally. The upper casing half weighs 410 kg — use rated lifting eyes only.

## 2. Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Flow range | 300 – 1,400 m³/h |
| Best efficiency point (BEP) | 950 m³/h at 38 m head |
| Motor | 75 kW, 4-pole, 1,485 rpm (50 Hz) |
| Mechanical seals | 2 × MS-400 (one per shaft end) |
| Bearings | 2 × BRG-6312, C3 |
| NPSH required at BEP | 4.6 m |
| Maximum working pressure | 12 bar |
| Weight (pump only) | 1,160 kg |

## 3. Installation notes that differ from the KP-250

* Foundation: steel frame permitted; natural frequency of the frame must be at least 25% away from running speed (24.75 Hz).
* Alignment tolerance: parallel ≤ **0.08 mm**, angular ≤ **0.06 mm per 100 mm**.
* Suction: **double-suction** — install at least **8 × pipe diameter** of straight pipe before the inlet, and never place an elbow in the plane of the shaft directly before the inlet (it causes uneven flow to the two impeller eyes, vibration, and axial thrust).

## 4. Operating limits (flexible foundation)

| Parameter | Normal | Alert | Alarm / shutdown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bearing housing vibration (RMS, 10–1000 Hz) | ≤ **3.5 mm/s** | 3.5 – **7.1** mm/s | > **7.1 mm/s** |
| Bearing housing temperature | ≤ 85 °C | 85 – 100 °C | > **100 °C** |
| Motor current | ≤ 132 A at 400 V | 100 – 110% | > 110% |

## 5. Maintenance schedule

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Record vibration, bearing temperatures, suction and discharge pressures |
| Every **3,000 operating hours** | Regrease each bearing with **25 g of LUB-EP2** |
| Annually | Check alignment; inspect both mechanical seals; check casing split-line bolts torque (**420 N·m**, M30) |
| Every 24,000 hours | Replace bearings (BRG-6312 × 2) and both seals (MS-400 × 2); inspect impeller and wear rings (max clearance 0.9 mm) |

## 6. Troubleshooting specific to split-case pumps

| Symptom | Probable cause | Corrective action |
|---|---|---|
| Vibration at 1× with high axial thrust | Uneven flow to impeller eyes (elbow before the suction inlet) | Re-pipe with a straight run or flow straightener |
| Cavitation noise at partial load | Suction recirculation below 40% of BEP flow | Keep flow above **380 m³/h** (40% BEP) |
| Leakage at the split line | Gasket damaged or bolts under-torqued | Replace split-line gasket; torque 420 N·m in cross pattern |
| Bearing temperature high on one side only | Axial thrust from unbalanced suction or worn wear ring on one side | Inspect wear rings; correct suction piping |

For general causes of low flow, cavitation, seal leakage, and overloads, the tables in IOM-KP250 §7 apply.
