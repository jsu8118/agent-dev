# Shipping and Delivery (Policy SHP-003, revision 2026-02)

## 1. Lead times

* **In-stock items** ship within **2 business days** of order confirmation.
* **Built-to-order pumps** ship according to the product lead time (KP-250-X: 35 days, KP-400: 42 days, KP-600: 56 days) from order confirmation.
* **Expedited processing** is available for a surcharge of 10% of the order value (minimum $150), subject to capacity.

## 2. Carriers

| Carrier | Use |
|---|---|
| NorthLine Freight | Pallets / LTL freight, domestic (all pumps over 30 kg) |
| SwiftParcel | Parcels under 30 kg, domestic |
| BlueRiver Logistics | International shipments (EU, UK, APAC) |

## 3. Terms

* Domestic: FCA Kestrel warehouse. International: DAP (named place) unless otherwise agreed.
* Tracking numbers are sent in the ship confirmation email and are visible in *Kestrel Connect*.
* Delivery address changes are possible **until the order ships**.

## 4. Delays and exceptions

* Kestrel notifies the customer within **1 business day** of a carrier exception (weather, customs hold, damage, address problem).
* **Customs holds** on EU shipments typically clear in 2–4 business days; the consignee's **EORI number** is required.
* If a shipment arrives **more than 5 business days after the promised date** for reasons within Kestrel's control, the customer is entitled, on request, to a **refund of the freight charge** or **free expedited freight on their next order**.
* Delays caused by weather, customs, or incorrect addresses supplied by the customer are not within Kestrel's control.

## 5. What support should tell customers about late shipments

1. Look up the shipment status, carrier, tracking number, and ETA.
2. If there is a carrier exception, explain it plainly and give the updated ETA if known.
3. If the delay entitles the customer to compensation under §4, say so and offer the options.
4. Never guess an ETA that isn't in the system; offer to follow up instead.
