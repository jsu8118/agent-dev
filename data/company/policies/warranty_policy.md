# Kestrel Standard Limited Warranty (Policy WAR-001, revision 2026-03)

## 1. Coverage periods

Warranty periods start on the **ship date** recorded in Atlas ERP.

| Product line | SKU prefix | Warranty period |
|---|---|---|
| Pumps | KP- | **24 months** |
| Valves | KV- | **18 months** |
| Controllers | KC- | **12 months** |
| Sensors | VS-, PT-, FT- | **12 months** |
| Spare parts, seal kits, wear parts | MS-, IMP-, BRG-, CPL-, GSK-, ORK-, LUB-, FLT- | **6 months** |
| Services (installation, alignment) | SVC- | **90 days** (workmanship) |

A repaired or replaced item is covered for the **remainder of the original period or 90 days, whichever is longer**.

## 2. What is covered

Defects in materials and workmanship that appear under normal use, when the product is installed, operated, and maintained according to its Installation, Operation & Maintenance (IOM) manual.

## 3. Exclusions (not covered)

1. **Dry running** — operation without liquid in the pump. Typical evidence: heat-checked or cracked seal faces, burned elastomers, discolored shaft sleeve. Controllers with dry-run protection log fault **F10**.
2. **Cavitation damage** caused by insufficient NPSH available at the customer site (pitting on the impeller eye, noise "like gravel").
3. **Improper installation or alignment** by a non-certified installer. (Installations performed by Kestrel under SVC-INSTALL are covered.)
4. Use of **non-genuine parts**.
5. Pumping fluids outside the **materials compatibility chart** for the ordered construction.
6. **Electrical damage** from a supply outside ±10% of rated voltage, or from lightning.
7. **Normal wear parts** (mechanical seals, bearings, O-rings, gaskets, impeller wear rings) after the 6-month wear-part period.
8. Products **modified** without Kestrel's written approval.

## 4. How to make a claim

1. Customer (or Kestrel support on their behalf) opens a warranty claim with: order ID, SKU, serial number, date of failure, description of the failure, and photos if available.
2. Kestrel support checks the warranty period and obvious exclusions, then issues an **RMA** (Returns Merchandise Authorization) within **2 business days**.
3. For claims within warranty, Kestrel pays return freight.
4. Kestrel repairs or replaces the product at its option after inspection. Stocked replacements ship within **5 business days** of RMA approval.
5. **Advance replacement** (shipping the replacement before the failed unit is returned) is available for **Strategic-tier** customers, or for any customer when the failure is a **P1 critical-service outage**.
6. If inspection finds an exclusion applies, the customer is offered a repair quote; the claim is closed as *rejected* with the reason.

## 5. Limits

* Kestrel's liability is limited to repair, replacement, or refund of the purchase price of the defective product.
* Consequential damages (lost production, downtime costs, third-party labor) are **not** covered.
* Support staff and AI assistants must **not** promise compensation beyond this policy and must **not** admit liability or speculate in writing about root cause before inspection.
