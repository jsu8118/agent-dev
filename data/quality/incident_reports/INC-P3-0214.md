# Incident INC-P3-0214 — Failure analysis: solder voids on VD-2607-C boards

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Plant | P3 Hillview |
| Date | 2026-08-28 |
| Reported by | L. Novak; FA lab (external) |
| Category | Quality — supplier |
| Severity | High |
| Parts / lots | VoltDrive Electronics lot **VD-2607-C** |

## Description
X-ray inspection of the 5 failed boards shows **solder voids above 35% under the MCU thermal pad** (limit 25%). Voids raise MCU temperature under load, causing watchdog resets (F20 0x3A) at high ambient temperature. 3 more units failed the extended burn-in. 214 KC-2 units with VD-2607-C boards were already shipped to customers between 2026-08-01 and 2026-08-19.

## Immediate actions
* Lot VD-2607-C on hold (QH-2311); SCAR-1115 to VoltDrive.
* Field risk: units in hot environments (> 35 °C ambient) may show intermittent F20 errors.

## Status
Open. Customer communication plan pending.
