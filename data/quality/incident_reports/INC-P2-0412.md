# Incident INC-P2-0412 — Seal weep during hydrostatic test (KP-250)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Plant | P2 Lakeside (pump assembly & test) |
| Date | 2026-08-11 |
| Reported by | S. Brandt, Test cell lead |
| Category | Quality — test failure |
| Severity | Medium |
| Parts / lots | KP-250-S pumps; mechanical seal kits **MS-250**, supplier Precision Seals GmbH, lot **PS-2608-B** |

## Description
3 KP-250-S pumps failed the hydrostatic test (24 bar, 10 min) with a slow weep at the mechanical seal gland after 6–8 minutes. Historical first-pass failure rate for this test is 0.4%; this week it is 3 of 41 (7.3%).

## Immediate actions
* Seals replaced with kits from the same lot (only lot in stock); 2 of 3 then passed.
* Assembly torque on gland bolts re-checked: within specification.

## Status
Open — monitoring. Test cell asked Quality to review.
