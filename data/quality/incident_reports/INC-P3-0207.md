# Incident INC-P3-0207 — KC-2 controllers failing burn-in with F20

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Plant | P3 Hillview (controls) |
| Date | 2026-08-20 |
| Reported by | L. Novak, Electronics test lead |
| Category | Quality — test failure |
| Severity | Medium |
| Parts / lots | KC-2 main boards, supplier VoltDrive Electronics, lot **VD-2607-C** |

## Description
5 of 60 KC-2 units failed the 48-hour burn-in at 45 °C with intermittent fault **F20 (internal error, sub-code 0x3A: MCU watchdog reset)**. Normal burn-in fallout is under 1%. All 5 failed units have main boards from lot VD-2607-C.

## Immediate actions
* Failed boards sent to failure analysis.
* Burn-in extended to 72 hours for units with VD-2607-C boards.

## Status
Open.
