# Incident INC-P1-0318 — Porosity in KP-400 casing castings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Plant | P1 Riverside (machining) |
| Date | 2026-08-18 |
| Reported by | M. Okafor, Machining supervisor |
| Category | Quality — incoming material |
| Severity | Medium |
| Parts / lots | KP-400 lower casing castings, supplier Ferrous Castings Inc., lot **FC-2608-11** |

## Description
During finish machining of the split-line face, 3 of 14 lower casings from lot FC-2608-11 showed surface porosity (pinholes up to 1.5 mm) after the second pass. One casing leaked during the P1 casing pressure pre-test at 18 bar.

## Immediate actions
* Remaining 11 castings from FC-2608-11 placed on quality hold (tag QH-2291).
* Supplier corrective action request SCAR-1107 issued to Ferrous Castings.

## Status
Open. Supplier investigating pouring temperature records. No castings from this lot have shipped to P2.
