## Description
About 40 L of water-soluble coolant leaked from a cracked return hose on CNC cell 7 and reached the floor drain grate. The drain leads to the plant's oil-water separator; no release to the environment.

## Immediate actions
* Hose replaced; spill absorbed; separator checked by EHS.

Closed 2026-08-28.
