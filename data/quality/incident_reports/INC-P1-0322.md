## Description
A forklift reversing out of bay 3 passed within 1 m of an operator walking outside the marked pedestrian lane. The reversing alarm was working; the pedestrian lane marking was partly covered by pallets of impeller blanks.

## Immediate actions
* Pallets relocated; lane repainted; toolbox talk held with all shifts.
* Added convex mirror at the bay 3 exit.
